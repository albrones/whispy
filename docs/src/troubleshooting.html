Troubleshooting

Choose what you see, then open the likely causes. Each cause matches a check in make doctor, so you can confirm the fix.

On macOS, Whispy V2 has no make doctor yet: its menu names the missing permission, and ~/.whispy/v2/whispy2.log says what went wrong. The causes below that mention a port, a token or ~/.whispy.log are V1's and apply to Linux.

From the Whispy source folder, run the full diagnostic at any time:

make doctor
$troubleshooting

Still stuck? Read ~/.whispy/v2/whispy2.log~/.whispy.log and ~/.whispy-error.log, or open an issue.
