Troubleshooting

Choose what you see, then open the likely causes. Whispy says what went wrong in its log, one line per dictation (never its text).

tail -f ~/.whispy/v2/whispy2.log

Its menu also names a missing permission, with a row that opens the right System Settings pane.

Ask it what it is doing, and read the service's own output:

whispy2 send status
journalctl --user -u whispy2 -e
$troubleshooting

Still stuck? Read ~/.whispy/v2/whispy2.log, or open an issue.
