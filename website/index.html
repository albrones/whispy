Speak. It types.
Nothing leaves your computer.

Whispy is a private voice-dictation utility for macOS and Linux. Hold your push-to-talk key, talk, and release — or flip on toggle mode and press once to start, once to stop — your words land in whatever app you're using. All transcription runs locally with NVIDIA's parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3. No cloud, no account, no telemetry.

Why Whispy

100% local

Audio is transcribed on-device with Parakeet. Your voice never touches a server.

One key, anywhere

Hold your push-to-talk key to record, release to insert text into any focused field — terminal, browser, editor.

Hold or toggle

Not every dictation fits under a held-down key. Flip on toggle mode and one press starts recording, the next press stops it — composable with any trigger.

macOS & Linux

Native on macOS and Linux (X11). OS-specific seams sit behind a ports-and-adapters layer bound at runtime.

Knows what you're speaking

25 European languages, detected automatically — even when you switch mid-sentence. Nothing to configure.

Lives in the tray

Runs quietly as a background daemon in the menu bar (macOS) or system tray (Linux). No window to manage — flip Start at login and it's there every boot.

Fast enough to forget

A two-second phrase transcribes in about 75 ms on an M1 Pro. Text lands as you release the key.

Quiet when you are

Tap the key by accident, or hold it in a noisy room, and nothing gets typed. Two gates — loudness, then voice detection — keep non-speech away from the model instead of letting it answer with a stray “Yeah.”

How it works

  1. 1

    Hold your key

    A soft chime confirms Whispy is recording — Fn on macOS, Right Ctrl on Linux by default.

  2. 2

    Speak

    Whispy listens — the tray shows it's live (macOS adds an on-screen waveform).

  3. 3

    Release

    Whispy transcribes locally and types the text into the active field.

Prefer not to hold the key? Turn on Toggle mode in Settings — one press starts, the next stops.

One model, no choices

Whispy ships a single model and downloads it on first use. There is no size to pick and no language to set.

Modelnvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3, int8 ONNX, CPU

Memory~1.4 GB resident while loaded
Languages25, detected automatically — Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, Russian, Ukrainian

Speech outside those 25 languages will not transcribe. The list comes from the model card.

Install in one line

Runs on macOS and Linux (X11). Audio capture uses sounddevice/PortAudio — no sox needed.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/albrones/whispy/main/scripts/bootstrap.sh | bash

On macOS this builds and installs the signed Whispy.app (needs the Xcode Command Line Tools). Autostart is the in-app "Start at login" toggle.

On Linux, install the system deps first (X11 session required — Wayland users pick an "Xorg"/"X11" session at login):

# Debian/Ubuntu — xdotool (text injection), xclip (optional clipboard), PortAudio
sudo apt install xdotool xclip libportaudio2
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/albrones/whispy/main/scripts/bootstrap.sh | bash
